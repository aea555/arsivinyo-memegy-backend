# Memegy Terms of Service and Community Content Policy

## 1. Eligibility and Acceptance
By using Memegy, you confirm you are at least 18 years old and legally able to accept these terms.
You must provide accurate account information and keep your account secure.

## 2. User-Generated Content Responsibility
Users are solely responsible for videos they upload and share.
Memegy does not pre-screen all content and is not responsible for user-uploaded material.
Memegy may moderate, remove, restrict, or preserve content to enforce policy and legal obligations.

## 3. Prohibited Content
The following content is prohibited:

- Pornography or explicit sexual acts.
- Child sexual abuse material, child sexual exploitation, or any sexualized minor content.
- Rape glorification, sexual coercion advocacy, or non-consensual sexual content.
- Pedophilic, zoophilic/bestiality, necrophilic, or similarly extreme sexual content.
- Content that promotes or advertises controlled or illegal substances.
- Malicious, manipulative, deceptive, or exploitative content intended to cause harm.
- Overly graphic/disturbing content, including depictions of murder, severe injury, or corpses.
- Violent sexual assault content.
- Content violating applicable law.

## 4. NSFW Tagging Rules
Uploaders must set NSFW flags accurately.
Mis-tagging NSFW content is a policy violation.
NSFW may include, but is not limited to, excessive profanity, suggestive content, partial nudity, racism, dark humor, or partially disturbing content.

## 5. Enforcement
Memegy may apply one or more actions:

- Warning.
- Video quarantine (hidden from public surfaces).
- Video removal.
- Temporary account suspension.
- Permanent account ban.

Repeat or severe violations may result in immediate stronger action.

## 6. Reporting and Appeals
Users can report content they believe violates these rules.
Moderation decisions may include quarantine, removal, or account actions.
Uploaders may request review of moderation outcomes through support channels.

## 7. Law Enforcement and Legal Cooperation
Memegy may preserve records and report content to authorities where required or appropriate, including severe exploitation and violence categories.
Memegy may cooperate with lawful investigations and legal requests.

## 8. Jurisdiction and Updates
These terms are operated with US and Turkey compliance priorities and a global legal cooperation baseline.
Memegy may update these terms at any time by publishing a new version.
Continued use requires acceptance of the currently active terms version.
